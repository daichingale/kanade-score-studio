Kanade modifications: selected every third available semitone plus endpoints from
12 pre-rendered instrument bundles; extracted base64 MP3 payloads into local JSON;
retained source URL and SHA-256 per file. Audio bytes are unchanged. Playback may
resample pitches, loop sustained instruments and apply a volume envelope.
No endorsement by the original authors or packaging authors is implied.

Euphonium currently uses the tuba samples as an explicitly labelled substitute.
Marching percussion uses original procedural audio and is not part of FluidR3.
